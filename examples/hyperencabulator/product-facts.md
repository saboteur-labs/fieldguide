# Saboteur HyperEncabulator HX-1: product facts

This file is the source of truth for the example handbook. The HyperEncabulator is
fictional, so there is no running product to check claims against. Every factual
statement in `handbook/` must trace to a fact below, the same way a real guide's
statements trace to the running software. If a page says something this file does
not, the page is wrong.

It is not part of the guide and is never rendered.

## What it is

- F-1: The HX-1 supplies steady, balanced current to other workshop machines that are
  sensitive to drift. Marketing calls this "inverse reactive current for unilateral
  phase detractors".
- F-2: It sits on a bench or table. It does not mount on a wall.

## In the box

- F-3: The machine, a power cable, one amber bottle of bearing oil (250 ml), and an
  exhaust hose for the fan.

## Controls and indicators (labels as printed on the machine)

- F-4: **MAIN**: the power switch on the back, marked I (on) and O (off).
- F-5: **Diractance**: the large dial on the front, with three zones: **Low**,
  **Nominal**, **High**. It sets how much current the machine puts out.
- F-6: **Prefabulate**: a round button below the dial.
- F-7: **Marzlevane**: an indicator lamp above the dial.
  - Steady green: ready.
  - Blinking amber: warming up.
  - Steady red: a fault. The display shows a code when it knows the cause.
- F-8: **Waneshaft Lock**: a lever on the right side, with two positions: **Locked**
  and **Free**.
- F-9: A small display next to the lamp shows error codes (F-20 to F-22). It is blank
  otherwise.
- F-10: **Spurving bearing oil window**: a clear window on the left side with a fill
  line. The filler cap is above it.
- F-11: **Panametric fan outlet**: a round outlet on the back that the exhaust hose
  attaches to.

## Setting up

- F-12: Place it on a flat, level surface, with at least 30 cm of clear space on
  every side.
- F-13: Attach the exhaust hose to the Panametric fan outlet and lead the other end
  outside or to an open window.
- F-14: Before the first start, fill the bearing oil to the fill line using the
  included bottle. One bottle fills it from empty.
- F-15: Set Waneshaft Lock to **Locked** before every start. The machine will not
  start with it at **Free** (it shows E3, see F-22).

## Starting and stopping

- F-16: To start: plug in the power cable, turn the Diractance dial to **Low**, switch MAIN to I, then press
  Prefabulate once. The Marzlevane lamp blinks amber for about 90 seconds, then turns
  steady green. Then turn the dial to **Nominal**.
- F-17: **Nominal** is the setting for everyday use. **High** is only for machines
  whose own manual asks for it.
- F-18: To stop: turn the dial to **Low**, wait 10 seconds, then switch MAIN to O.
- F-19: Never move Waneshaft Lock to **Free** while MAIN is on.

## Error codes

- F-20: **E1**: the casing is too warm. Switch off, check the 30 cm clearance and the
  exhaust hose, let it cool for 30 minutes, then start again.
- F-21: **E2**: the bearing oil is low. Switch off and fill to the line.
- F-22: **E3**: Waneshaft Lock is at **Free**. Set it to **Locked** and start again.
- F-23: Steady red lamp with no code: switch off, wait one minute, and start again.
  If it happens again, contact Saboteur support.

## Care and limits

- F-24: Check the oil window once a week. Fill to the line if the oil is below it.
- F-25: Clean the casing with a dry cloth only. No water or cleaning sprays.
- F-26: Run it for at most 8 hours at a time, then let it rest, switched off, for
  30 minutes.
- F-27: It works in rooms between 10 °C and 35 °C.
