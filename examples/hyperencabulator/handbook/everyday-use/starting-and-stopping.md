# Starting and stopping

## Start

1. Check that **Waneshaft Lock** is at **Locked**.
2. Turn the **Diractance** dial to **Low**.
3. Switch **MAIN** to **I**.
4. Press **Prefabulate** once.
5. Wait for the **Marzlevane** light to change from blinking amber to steady green,
   about 90 seconds.
6. Turn the dial to **Nominal**.

## Stop

1. Turn the **Diractance** dial to **Low**.
2. Wait 10 seconds.
3. Switch **MAIN** to **O**.

## Good to know

- Never move **Waneshaft Lock** to **Free** while the machine is switched on.
- Run the HX-1 for no more than 8 hours at a time. Then switch it off and let it rest
  for 30 minutes before you start it again.
- It works in rooms between 10 °C and 35 °C.
