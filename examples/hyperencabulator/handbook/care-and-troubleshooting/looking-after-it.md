# Looking after it

## Check the oil every week

Look at the **Spurving bearing oil window** on the left side. If the oil is below the
line, open the filler cap and add oil until it reaches the line.

## Keep it clean

Wipe the outside with a dry cloth. Don't use water or cleaning sprays.
