# Error codes

When something is wrong, the **Marzlevane** light turns steady red. If the HX-1 knows
what the problem is, a code appears on the small display next to the light.

## E1: too warm

1. Switch **MAIN** to **O**.
2. Check that there is at least 30 cm of space on every side, and that the exhaust
   hose is attached and not blocked.
3. Let the machine cool for 30 minutes, then start it again.

## E2: low on oil

Switch **MAIN** to **O**, then add oil until it reaches the line in the
**Spurving bearing oil window**. See [Looking after it](looking-after-it.md#check-the-oil-every-week).

## E3: waneshaft not locked

Move **Waneshaft Lock** to **Locked**, then start again.

## Red light, no code

Switch **MAIN** to **O**, wait one minute, then start again. If the light turns red
again, contact Saboteur support.
