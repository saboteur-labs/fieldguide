# Setting up

## What's in the box

- The HyperEncabulator HX-1
- A power cable
- An amber bottle of bearing oil
- An exhaust hose

## Choose a spot

Put the HX-1 on a flat, level bench or table. Leave at least 30 cm of empty space on
every side, so air can move around it. It isn't made to hang on a wall.

## Connect the exhaust hose

The round outlet on the back is the **Panametric fan outlet**. Push one end of the
exhaust hose onto it. Lead the other end outside, or to an open window.

## Add the oil

The clear window on the left side is the **Spurving bearing oil window**. It has a
line marked on it.

1. Open the filler cap above the window.
2. Pour in oil from the amber bottle until it reaches the line. One bottle fills it
   from empty.
3. Close the cap.

## Lock the waneshaft

Find the **Waneshaft Lock** lever on the right side and move it to **Locked**. The
HX-1 won't start unless this lever is at **Locked**.
